# Menopause Skincare Guide: Free Starter

An interactive introduction to skin care for women in perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause. It explains what menopause does to skin, hair and nails, what skin care can and cannot do, why the market is so confusing, and how to judge the evidence behind any claim.

The skill is evidence-based and informed by the personal experience of two real people, Dani and Kevin. They are not medical or skin care professionals, and the products they use are described as their own experience, not as recommendations.

## What is included

- Part 1: what menopause does to your skin, and what skin care can and cannot do
- Six questions to ask about any product, and four plain evidence labels
- A short guided walk-through that ends with a simple written routine
- Essential safety guidance, including when to see a clinician
- A short reference list

## How to use it

Install the plugin, start a new chat, and try one of these:

- "I'm in perimenopause and my skin has changed. Where do I begin?"
- "What can skin care actually do for menopausal skin?"
- "How do I judge whether a skin care claim is backed by evidence?"

The guide will ask a few questions about you, then explain what applies to your situation.

## Data and privacy

This plugin is a set of written instructions only. It does not collect, store or send any data, and it does not connect to any outside service.

## Troubleshooting and support

If the guide does not respond as described, start a new chat and check that the plugin is enabled. For questions or problems, open an issue at https://github.com/Kev-Dani/menopause-skincare-starter/issues

## Please note

This is an AI-powered, self-guided skill. You are not communicating with Dani or Kevin, and it does not include direct access to them. It provides educational information only. It is not medical or dermatological advice, diagnosis or treatment. See a doctor, dermatologist or pharmacist about any skin problem that is new, changing, painful or not improving, and before starting, stopping or changing any prescription product. No results are guaranteed.
